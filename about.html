About Wizard Digitech

Digital Thinking. Technology Solutions. Practical Support.

A Vijayawada-based business bringing together digital, creative and technology solutions under one brand.

Who We Are

Wizard Digitech was founded with the vision of bringing together technology expertise and digital capabilities under one platform.

T.V. Amarnadh, Founder, leads the Technology Solutions side of the business, while T.S.L. Nishitha, Proprietor, leads the Digital Solutions side.

Together, they bring complementary expertise across technology and digital solutions, with a shared focus on understanding customer requirements and providing practical and dependable support.

At Wizard Digitech, we believe that good solutions begin with understanding. Our approach is centred on listening to what our customers need, identifying the right direction and delivering solutions that are useful, reliable and relevant.
